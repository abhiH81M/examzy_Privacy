Privacy Policy

Exam Maker: Offline Tests is local-first. Optional features use cloud services for online classrooms, AI question generation, subscriptions, advertising, and analytics. We do not sell personal information.

Last Updated: August 24, 2026
Effective: November 25, 2025
Version: 4.0.125

This policy explains what Exam Maker: Offline Tests (also called “Examzy”, “we”, “us”, or “the app”) processes, why it is processed, and the choices available to you. It applies to the mobile app and its optional online features.

01 — Local Data

Local-first exam creation

Creating, editing, printing, exporting, and conducting an offline exam is designed to work from your device. The app may store the following locally:

  • Exam questions and answers you create, saved drafts, and local exam progress
  • Student names, roll numbers, answers, scores, results, attendance, and poll responses that you enter or receive in a local classroom flow
  • App preferences, including your local display name, age range, consent choices, and settings
  • PDF, CSV, or other files that you choose to create, save, print, or share

We do not automatically upload this local content merely because you create it. If you share or export a file through another app, email service, cloud drive, printer, or messaging service, that recipient or service handles the shared file under its own practices.

02 — Online Classroom (Cloud) Data

When you choose to use Online Classroom

Online Classroom is optional and uses Google Firebase, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, and App Check.

Hosts: To host an online session, you sign in with Google or, on supported Apple devices, Sign in with Apple. We receive a Firebase account identifier and the basic account information the provider makes available, such as name, email address, and provider identifier. This identifies the host, protects the session, and connects any subscription entitlement to the correct account.

Participants: A participant joins with a host-provided QR code or join token. The app does not ask a participant to create a Google or Apple account. Firebase creates an anonymous technical identifier to secure the join and submission flow. The session may process the name or roll number entered by the participant, the session identifier, answer drafts and submissions, scores, timing, and participation status.

To deliver the online exam, we store session metadata and exam materials in Firestore. Question papers and answer material are encrypted before cloud storage; keys are separately protected and released only at the scheduled points needed to conduct and grade the session. The host can see the session results permitted by the feature.

We also process limited security and reliability information, such as Firebase App Check results, authentication state, rate-limit records, session-release status, and error categories. This helps prevent automated abuse, unauthorized session access, duplicate submissions, and fraudulent use of paid features.

03 — AI Question Generation

What is sent when you use Generate with AI

AI question generation is optional. When you request it, the app sends the topic or instructions you enter, selected difficulty, requested question count, and a request identifier through our Firebase Cloud Function to Firebase AI Logic and Google Gemini. The generated questions and answer options are returned to the app for you to review and save locally.

Please do not enter personal, confidential, student-identifying, health, financial, or other sensitive information into an AI prompt. AI generation is not needed to process ordinary local exams.

For reliability and abuse prevention, the service temporarily stores a request result, generation status, a Firebase account or anonymous identifier, and quota records. Verified rewarded-ad tickets are also stored briefly when an ad is required to unlock a free generation. These records prevent duplicate requests, enforce the current allowance, verify rewards, and protect the service; they are not a shared question bank.

04 — Advertising & Analytics

Advertising

The free version may show advertising through Google AdMob. We ask you to choose an age range on the device so we can request ads appropriate for that choice:

  • 13–17: the app requests non-personalized ads and applies the applicable under-age-of-consent setting where required.
  • 18+: the app may request personalized ads only where the consent framework permits it; otherwise it requests non-personalized ads or does not request an ad.

Google AdMob and its partners may process technical and advertising information, such as advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, device and operating-system information, app interactions with ads, diagnostics, and consent signals. We do not send your exam content, answers, student names, roll numbers, or results to AdMob for advertising.

Free AI generation may require a rewarded ad. The reward is authorized only after Google AdMob sends a signed server-to-server confirmation. We retain the opaque reward ticket and a hashed transaction reference only long enough to verify and prevent replay of that reward.

Paid plans remove in-app advertising. They do not control information independently processed by an app store, RevenueCat, or other services used for the purchase.

Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics for limited product analytics, such as app opens, screen or feature flow, online-session lifecycle, paywall entry, and PDF export type. Analytics events are designed not to include exam titles or content, answers, student names, roll numbers, participant identifiers, scores, or detailed device-integrity results.

Where the consent framework requires it, analytics stays off until you choose Allow. You can change that choice later in the app’s Privacy Choices. Outside those regions, analytics may be enabled after the app has resolved its consent state.

05 — Purchases and Subscriptions

How paid plans are handled

Subscriptions are sold and processed by the relevant app store, such as Google Play or the Apple App Store. We use RevenueCat to display offers, restore purchases, and receive purchase or entitlement status. RevenueCat is associated with a Firebase account identifier so our server can apply the correct Plus or Pro entitlement.

We do not receive your full payment-card number. We may retain the Firebase identifier, product or entitlement status, purchase event identifier, expiry or revocation information, and timestamps needed to provide paid features, prevent fraud, and handle support.

06 — Third-Party Services

Who we work with, and why

These providers process information under their own privacy terms. We use them only for the functions described in this policy:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, online-session data, Cloud Functions, App Check, Firebase AI Logic, and analytics
  • Google AdMob — advertising, consent management, ad measurement, and rewarded-ad verification
  • Google Sign-In and Google Play — optional sign-in, purchases, licensing, and Android platform services
  • Sign in with Apple and Apple App Store — optional iOS sign-in, purchases, and iOS platform services
  • RevenueCat — subscription offerings, purchase restoration, and entitlement status

07 — Retention and Deletion

How long data is kept

  • Local data: stays on your device until you delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Online Classroom data: is kept for the retention period assigned to the host’s tier and shown when the session is created. That period is snapshotted for the session, then an automated server cleanup removes expired sessions and their associated cloud records. Cleanup runs daily, so deletion may not occur at the exact second the retention period ends.
  • AI retry and reward records: expire automatically after their short operational period. AI usage records are retained for the applicable quota window; an anonymous guest-usage counter may remain associated with that anonymous Firebase identity so the one-time guest allowance cannot be repeatedly reset.
  • Account, security, and purchase records: are retained while needed to provide the account or entitlement, prevent fraud, resolve support issues, or comply with legal obligations.

Third-party providers may retain data under their own policies and legal requirements.

08 — Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Access, correction, and deletion

We do not sell or rent personal information. You may ask about, access, correct, or request deletion of cloud data we control by contacting us at the address below. We may need information to identify the relevant account or session and to verify the request.

You can delete local exams, results, and other local data in the app where available, or clear app storage/uninstall the app. You can change the device age range, analytics choice, and required ad privacy choices from Settings. You can also choose not to use Online Classroom or AI generation if you do not want those features to send data to their cloud providers. Signing out of a provider account on the device does not by itself delete cloud data or an active subscription record.

Our service providers operate globally, so information may be processed in countries other than the one where you live. Where applicable law gives you additional privacy rights, you may use the contact below to exercise them.

09 — Children and Student Use

Teacher-supervised use

The app is not designed for children under 13. It may be used by students in a teacher-, school-, parent-, guardian-, or institution-supervised classroom setting. A host should collect only the participant information genuinely needed for that session, such as a nickname or roll number, and obtain any required notices or permissions.

We do not knowingly seek to collect unnecessary personal information from children. If you believe a child provided personal information through the app without appropriate authorization, contact us so we can review and, where appropriate, delete the cloud data we control.

10 — Security

Safeguards

We use reasonable technical safeguards appropriate to the feature, including encrypted online exam material, provider authentication, Firebase App Check, access rules, server-side entitlement checks, and signed rewarded-ad verification. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; keep session QR codes and join tokens private and share them only with intended participants.

11 — Policy Updates

How we handle changes

We may update this policy as the app, its services, or applicable requirements change. We will update the “Last Updated” date when we do. If a material change requires notice or consent, we will provide it as required.
