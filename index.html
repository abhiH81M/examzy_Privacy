Privacy Policy

Exam Maker: Offline Tests respects your privacy. Local data stays on your device; Online Classroom uses Firebase for the cloud session described below.

Last Updated: Jul 18, 2026
Effective: Nov 25, 2025
Version: v3.6.0

We respect your privacy.

This policy explains, in simple words, what Exam Maker: Offline Tests stores and why. Local drafts and settings stay on your device. Online Classroom uses Firebase to run the cloud session described below.

01 - Local Data

Local and online classroom data

The app stores the following on your device:

  • Exam questions and answers you create
  • Saved drafts and local exam progress
  • Student results and scores
  • Poll responses collected on-device
  • App preferences and settings
  • Profile details you enter such as name, roll number, and age range

Online Classroom (Beta, currently limited to 100 students per session) is different. To let a teacher and students connect over the internet, the app uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Google Login is required for teachers to manage their sessions across devices and support future subscription plans. When teachers use Google Login, the app receives basic profile information needed to create and verify their account.

Students do not need to create permanent accounts. They participate seamlessly using a session identifier. To process and deliver results, these student identifiers and their exam interactions are securely maintained under the hosting teacher's account. The app processes this identifier, the participant name and roll number entered for that session, encrypted question data, submitted answer selections, session timing, and release status. If a student later decides to host their own exams, they simply create a teacher account.

Online question papers are encrypted with a unique AES-256-GCM key before upload. The teacher keeps the paper key and correct-answer map in secure local storage until the scheduled release. Submitted participants can receive the released answers to calculate results locally; participants who do not submit cannot read them.

Online Classroom data is retained for the free session period and expires 24 hours after the submission grace period. The teacher app removes expired session data when the teacher next opens the app. If a teacher never returns, an abandoned session can remain until that cleanup occurs.

02 - Advertising

Advertising data (free version only)

The free version displays ads via Google AdMob.

We ask for an age range so ads can be handled more safely and appropriately for this device. The app uses these ad settings:

  • 13-17: non-personalized ads with underage protections and teen content limits
  • 18+: personalized ads when consent rules allow it; otherwise the app falls back to non-personalized requests when available

We do not use exam content, answers, student names, roll numbers, or results to choose ads.

Analytics follows the same regional consent rules.

In regions where the consent framework requires it, we ask separately for analytics consent before Firebase Analytics collection starts. Analytics is limited to aggregated app behavior, screen views, and product funnels. We do not use exam content, answers, student names, roll numbers, or results for analytics targeting.

Google Firebase Analytics and its partners may receive technical analytics information such as app opens, screen views, feature usage, device model, OS version, and other coarse app-usage signals.

Ad placements:

  • Student dashboard: banner ads may appear at the top or bottom of the home screen
  • Student joining flow: banner ads may appear on the student information screen before starting an exam
  • Student waiting/submission flow: banner or inline ads may appear while waiting or after submission screens; an interstitial may appear only around waiting/submission completion
  • Student poll/result review screens: banner ads may appear around the screen content
  • Teacher screens: banner ads may appear on teacher dashboard, exam setup, sharing, results, analytics, and draft screens
  • Teacher premium unlocks: optional rewarded ads may appear when a teacher chooses to unlock an export or premium feature without purchasing Premium

Google AdMob and its partners may receive technical ad-related information such as:

  • Device advertising ID (GAID on Android, IDFA on iOS)
  • IP address and approximate location (country/region level)
  • Device model and OS version
  • App usage signals for ad relevance

Premium users: No advertisements. No ad-related data collection through in-app ads.

You can change the saved age range later from app settings.

Third-party services

The developers are not liable for dropped connections, delayed transfers, cloud data loss, local data loss, service interruptions, or consequences arising from using the app for high-stakes, official, grade-deciding, or standardized examinations.

04 - Data Removal

Local deletion and online-session retention

Most exam data is local. For Online Classroom, the teacher app deletes expired cloud sessions when the teacher next opens the app, as described above. We do not offer a separate cloud-data export workflow.

To remove local app data, clear the app storage, use the in-app delete-data tools where available, or uninstall the app from your device.

05 - Controls

Device-based controls only

  • Buy Premium to remove all ads
  • Edit the saved age range from app settings
  • Review analytics consent and ad privacy options from app settings
  • Clear app data or uninstall the app from your device
  • Avoid creating or joining an Online Classroom session if you do not want its session details processed by Firebase

06 - Children

Children's privacy

Exam Maker: Offline Tests is intended for users ages 12 and up. The app does not request child-directed ads. For ad handling, the available age settings are 13-17 and 18+. Analytics follows the same regional consent rules and remains off until allowed where required. Local data is stored locally. Online Classroom processes only the session data described above so classmates can participate together.
