This policy explains, in plain language, what the app collects, why, and how you can control it.

01 — Local Data

What stays on your device

The app stores the following locally, on your device only:

  • Exam questions and answers you create
  • Saved drafts and local exam progress
  • Student results and scores
  • Poll responses collected on-device
  • App preferences and settings
  • Profile details you enter, such as name, roll number, and age range

02 — Online Classroom (Cloud) Data

What the cloud session processes

Online Classroom (Beta, currently limited to 100 students per session) works differently from local exams. To let a teacher and students connect over the internet, the app uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.

Teachers sign in with Google or Apple so sessions can be managed across devices and to support subscription plans. Sign-in provides the basic account information (such as name and email) needed to create and verify a teacher account.

Students do not create accounts. They join with a session identifier generated for that session. To run and deliver results, the app processes: that identifier, the participant name and roll number entered for the session, encrypted question data, submitted answer selections, session timing, and release status — all stored under the hosting teacher's account. A student who wants to host their own exams can create a teacher account separately.

Question papers are encrypted before upload using industry-standard encryption. The teacher's device holds the decryption key and the answer key in secure local storage until the scheduled release. Participants who submitted receive the release so they can see results; participants who did not submit do not.

Online Classroom data is retained for the free session period and is deleted 24 hours after the submission grace period ends. Expired sessions are removed automatically the next time the teacher opens the app. If a teacher never returns, an abandoned session remains until that cleanup runs — you can also request early deletion at any time (see "Your Privacy Rights" below).

03 — Advertising & Analytics

Free version only

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. We ask for an age range so ads are handled appropriately for the account holder. The app currently offers two settings for this purpose:

  • 13–17: non-personalized ads only, with additional teen-content protections
  • 18+: personalized ads where consent rules allow it, otherwise non-personalized ads

Exam Maker: Offline Tests is generally suitable for ages 12 and up, and its offline features never send data to us at all. We do not knowingly direct advertising at, or collect an advertising identifier from, a child we know to be under 13.

We never use exam content, answers, student names, roll numbers, or results to select ads or personalize the experience.

Where required by regional consent rules, we ask separately before Firebase Analytics starts collecting data. Analytics is limited to aggregated app behavior, screen views, and product usage — never exam content, answers, names, roll numbers, or results.

When ads are eligible to be shown under the rules above, Google AdMob and its partners may receive technical information such as:

  • Device advertising ID (GAID on Android, IDFA on iOS)
  • IP address and approximate location (country/region level)
  • Device model and OS version
  • App usage signals for ad relevance

Premium users: no advertisements, and no ad-related data collection through in-app ads.

You can change the saved age range at any time from app settings.

05 — Your Privacy Rights

Access, correction, and deletion

We do not sell personal information. You can request a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing us at the address below. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Because our service providers (Google Firebase and AdMob) operate globally, your data may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, under those providers' own security and data-handling commitments.

Depending on where you live, you may have specific rights under laws such as the GDPR (EEA/UK), the CCPA/CPRA (California), or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act — the contact below is how to exercise any of them.

06 — Children's Privacy

Independent accounts and supervised classroom use

Exam Maker: Offline Tests is generally designed for ages 12 and up. Local exam creation, taking exams, and offline polls never send any personal data to our servers, and are available at that age.

Online Classroom is different: creating a teacher account sends basic account information to our servers through Google or Apple Sign-In. Consistent with COPPA, creating that specific account requires being at least 13. Users younger than 13 can still use every offline feature of the app, and can join an Online Classroom session as a student without creating any account — see below.

Online Classroom sessions are created and controlled by a teacher for classroom use. Where a teacher or school includes participants younger than 13 in a session, that use is authorized and supervised by the teacher or institution, who is responsible for any parental notice or school policy their setting requires. Student participation in a session never requires creating an independent account.

We do not knowingly collect advertising identifiers from, or show personalized or behaviorally-targeted ads to, any participant identified as under 13. We never sell children's data, under any circumstances.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal data outside of a teacher-authorized classroom session, contact us at the email below and we will delete it.

For Online Classroom, expired cloud sessions are deleted automatically as described above. You can also request early deletion, or a copy, of your data at any time by emailing us — see "Your Privacy Rights."
