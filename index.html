Privacy Policy

Exam Maker: Offline Tests respects your privacy. The short version is simple: your exam data never leaves your device.

Last Updated: Jun 16, 2026
Effective: Nov 25, 2025
Version: v3.4.3

We respect your privacy.

This policy explains, in simple words, what Exam Maker: Offline Tests stores and why. Short version: your exam data stays on your device.

01 - Local Data

Data stored only on your device

The app stores the following on your device only:

  • Exam questions and answers you create
  • Saved drafts and local exam progress
  • Student results and scores
  • Poll responses collected on-device
  • App preferences and settings
  • Profile details you enter such as name, roll number, and age range

We do not run user accounts or a server that stores your exam data. We do not have a cloud copy of your exams, results, or student details.

02 - Advertising

Advertising data (free version only)

The free version displays ads via Google AdMob.

We ask for an age range so ads can be handled more safely and appropriately for this device:

  • Under 13: child-directed, non-personalized ads with stricter content limits
  • 13-17: non-personalized ads with underage protections
  • 18+: standard AdMob requests

We do not use exam content, answers, student names, roll numbers, or results to choose ads.

Ad placements:

  • Student dashboard: banner ads may appear at the top or bottom of the home screen
  • Student joining flow: banner ads may appear on the student information screen before starting an exam
  • Student waiting/submission flow: banner or inline ads may appear while waiting or after submission screens; an interstitial may appear only around waiting/submission completion
  • Student poll/result review screens: banner ads may appear around the screen content
  • Teacher screens: banner ads may appear on teacher dashboard, exam setup, sharing, results, analytics, and draft screens
  • Teacher premium unlocks: optional rewarded ads may appear when a teacher chooses to unlock an export or premium feature without purchasing Premium

Google AdMob and its partners may receive technical ad-related information such as:

  • Device advertising ID (GAID on Android, IDFA on iOS)
  • IP address and approximate location (country/region level)
  • Device model and OS version
  • App usage signals for ad relevance

Premium users: No advertisements. No ad-related data collection through in-app ads.

You can change the saved age range later from app settings.

03 - Third Parties

Third-party services

We use third-party services only for the purposes listed below.

  • Google AdMob - Advertising (free version)
  • Google Play Services - In-app purchases and licensing
  • Nearby Connections - Peer-to-peer exam sharing on supported devices

Nearby sharing is device-to-device and depends on local connection availability and platform limits. If many students submit results at the same time, transfers may complete over multiple connection cycles instead of all at once.

04 - Data Removal

No server-side deletion workflow

Because we do not store your exam data on our servers, there is no server-side account deletion or export process.

To remove local app data, clear the app storage, use the in-app delete-data tools where available, or uninstall the app from your device.

05 - Controls

Device-based controls only

  • Buy Premium to remove all ads
  • Edit the saved age range from app settings
  • Clear app data or uninstall the app from your device

06 - Children

Children's privacy

Exam Maker: Offline Tests may be used by students of all ages under teacher supervision. We ask for an age range so ad requests can be handled more safely for younger users. Exam data is stored locally, and we do not send student exam answers or results to our own servers.

07 - Updates

Policy updates

When significant changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the top changes and material changes may trigger an in-app notification. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the current policy.
